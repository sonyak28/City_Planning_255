Interpretation

% no vehicle shows the strongest positive correlation with amenity count (rho = 0.476, q < 0.05). % non-white is negatively correlated (rho = -0.243, q < 0.05), indicating stations in majority non-white neighborhoods have fewer nearby services. Median income is not significantly associated (rho = -0.064, q = 0.575).

Unmet need index = % no-vehicle x (1 - normalized amenity count), min-max normalized across all stations.

Gini coefficient reference

A Gini of 0 = perfectly equal access; 1 = all amenities at one station. The network-wide Gini of 0.428 is comparable to income inequality in moderately unequal countries. The lower peripheral Gini (0.315) reflects that peripheral stations are uniformly low in access rather than internally unequal.
